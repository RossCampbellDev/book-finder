About Book Finder

Book Finder helps you locate books at local bookstores, libraries, and other retailers in your area.

For Readers

Search for books by ISBN, title, or author and see which stores near you have them in stock. View prices, conditions, and store locations on an interactive map.

For Bookstores

Register your store and manage your inventory online. Make it easy for local customers to find the books you have in stock.

Features

  • Search by ISBN, title, or author
  • Interactive map showing store locations
  • Location-based search to find nearby stores
  • Real-time inventory and pricing information
  • Easy inventory management for bookstores
